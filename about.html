Oyinkansola

Oyenuga Oyinkansola is THE VOICE — a poet, spoken word artist, writer and psychologist, carrying words like medicine across the nation.

“Water cannot drown a fish!”
Her Story

Rooted in Ogun, trained for souls

A native of Ogun State, Nigeria, Oyinkansola graduated with a degree in Psychology from Olabisi Onabanjo University — and chose the stage as her consulting room.

Her prowess in spoken word poetry has carried her to podiums of significant events across the nation. But the credentials were never the point. She does spoken word because of her passion for souls — convinced that words, placed carefully, can reach places therapy alone cannot touch.

She organizes a live recording every year called HEALING, where she takes her fan community through a therapeutic experience of poetry, worship and honest conversation about pain, faith and restoration.

The Creed
“Words have the power to fix broken souls.
And water cannot drown a fish.”
