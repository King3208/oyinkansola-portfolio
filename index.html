Meet the Voice

A psychologist who prescribes poetry

A native of Ogun State, Nigeria and a graduate of Psychology from Olabisi Onabanjo University, Oyinkansola does spoken word out of a passion for souls — believing every broken heart can be reached by the right words, at the right time.

The Annual Live Recording

HEALING — a therapeutic experience

Every year, Oyinkansola gathers her community for HEALING — a live recording that walks listeners through a therapeutic journey of words, worship and restoration. Tracks like Goodness of God have pulled listeners back from the edge.
